Four pre-existing ways the cold tier (spill files + cold index) loses a key, or brings back a deleted or overwritten one, around keyspace-level operations. Read first: `plans/WS15-durability-followups/{NOTES,SUMMARY}.md` (dead-slot ledger, promote-then-sweep, the crash-window argument) and `plans/WS19-cold-durability/{NOTES,SUMMARY}.md` (the moon#1231 unlink hold, `storage::tiered::unlink_hold`, the moon#1253 superseded set).

## Issues (in this order)
1. **moon#1254 (BUG, data loss).** `MOVE key db` on a spilled or in-flight key deletes it from both databases and answers `:0`: `src.remove(key)` in `move_cmd.rs` drops the cold entry and the in-flight record but returns `None`.
   - Fix direction: make the key hot first, the way `get` promotes a cold or in-flight key, then move the hot entry. A failed cold read refuses with `-IOERR` (moon#1225's rule), never a silent `:0`. `COPY … DB n` gets the same check. Cover every caller of `move_core` / the COPY core (connection handlers, SPSC, Lua bridge, MULTI).
   - Keep WS16's two-db capture (`snapshot_cow::capture_two_db`) and WS19's change counting (`move_core` wrapper) intact.
   - Tests: a lib test (spilled key and in-flight key → MOVE → key in destination with its value, gone from source; same for COPY DB) and a real-server test at `--shards 1` and 4 with `--maxmemory` + `--disk-offload enable`, across BGREWRITEAOF + kill -9 + restart.
2. **moon#1236 (BUG, resurrection).** A cold key overwritten hot with a TTL (`SET k new PX t`) leaves its old spill copy as a "cold shadow". After a restart past the TTL, recovery skips the expired new value and the cold-index rebuild brings back the OLD value (100/100 without a rewrite).
   - Fix direction (pick one, argue it in NOTES): the overwrite retires the shadow durably (record the dead slot as the moon#1215 ledger does, dropped at the next fold); or every recovery path that skips an expired entry (RDB load, AOF replay, fold base) tombstones the key's cold shadow. Check the other blind-overwrite paths (SET without TTL, SETEX, GETSET, RENAME onto a cold key, RESTORE REPLACE) for the same shadow.
   - Test: extend the crash-suite harness (`tests/crash_recovery_cold_del_rewrite.rs` or a sibling) — kill -9 and clean restart, with and without a rewrite, at `--shards 1` and 4. Add a per-PR lib/recovery-level test as well; the crash suite is `#[ignore]`d.
3. **moon#1237 (BUG).** `SWAPDB a b` swaps whole `Database` structures, cold indexes included, but spill files keep their manifest `db_index`. After a rewrite + restart, cold keys reappear in their OLD db and DELs made after the swap do not stick.
   - Decide between re-tagging (durable, crash-consistent with the AOF's SWAPDB record) and refusing SWAPDB while either db has cold data (explicit error + docs). Prefer the redis-compatible option; choose refuse only if you cannot prove the crash window for re-tag, and say why in NOTES. Cover every SWAPDB path (SPSC fan-out, handler_single, replica apply, AOF replay).
   - Test: spill, SWAPDB, DEL, BGREWRITEAOF, kill -9, restart — every key in the right db and no deleted key returns; `--shards 1` and 4, both runtimes.
4. **moon#1260 (BUG, data loss).** With `--appendonly no` there is no fold view, so the moon#1231 hold never applies: a cold key inherited from an earlier AOF run, read back into memory, loses its spill file to the orphan sweep and is gone after kill -9 (83/83).
   - Fix direction: without an AOF, the durable copy of an inherited cold key is its spill file until a snapshot captures it. Either hold zero-ref files below the boot generation until a successful BGSAVE that includes those keys, or have BGSAVE include cold keys. Prefer the hold (it stays inside `storage/tiered` + the sweep); if you choose the BGSAVE route, `src/persistence/snapshot.rs` belongs to WS21 — make it an isolated cross-ownership commit.
   - Test: the reviewer's `r4_no_aof_promoted_…` case (`/home/user/wt/handoff/review4_ws19_proofs.rs`), at `--shards 1` and 4, both runtimes.
